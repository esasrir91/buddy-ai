# CA copilot — India quick reference (verify against latest law)

> Original summary notes for grounding. Rates/thresholds change with each Finance
> Act and CBIC/CBDT notification — always confirm against the official source
> before relying on a number.

## GST essentials
- Standard rate slabs: 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28% (+ cess on some goods).
- Intra-state supply → CGST + SGST (each = half the rate). Inter-state → IGST (full rate).
- Key returns: GSTR-1 (outward supplies, ~11th), GSTR-3B (summary + payment, ~20th),
  GSTR-2B (auto-drafted ITC statement — reconcile purchases against it), GSTR-9 (annual).
- Input Tax Credit (ITC): claimable only if invoice appears in 2B, goods/services
  received, supplier has paid tax, and within the time limit. Reverse on non-payment in 180 days.
- Place of supply determines CGST+SGST vs IGST.

## TDS (resident payees) — indicative
- 192 Salary (slab rates), 194A Interest (10%), 194C Contractor (1% indiv/HUF, 2% others),
  194H Commission, 194I Rent (2% plant/machinery, 10% land/building),
  194J Professional (10%) / Technical (2%), 194Q Purchase of goods (0.1% over ₹50L).
- Higher TDS u/s 206AB for non-filers; 20% if PAN not furnished (194Q etc.).
- Deposit by 7th of next month (March by 30th April); file 24Q/26Q quarterly.

## Financial statements (Companies Act, 2013 — Schedule III)
- Balance Sheet: Equity & Liabilities (Equity, Non-current, Current) and Assets
  (Non-current, Current). P&L: Revenue, Other income, Expenses, Tax, PAT.
- Apply AS or Ind AS depending on company class. Cash Flow per AS 3 / Ind AS 7.
- Common ratios: current, quick, debt-equity, interest coverage, net margin, ROE, asset turnover.

## Audit (Standards on Auditing — SA)
- Plan → assess risk (SA 315) → materiality (SA 320) → controls testing →
  substantive procedures (vouching, verification, analytical SA 520) → conclude & report (SA 700 series).
- Working papers must evidence the basis of every conclusion (SA 230).
- Sampling (SA 530): define population, method (random/systematic), sample size, evaluate exceptions.

## Compliance calendar (recurring)
- TDS/TCS payment 7th; GSTR-1 11th; GSTR-3B 20th; PF & ESI 15th; advance tax 15 Jun/Sep/Dec/Mar.
- ROC: AOC-4 (financials) and MGT-7 (annual return) after AGM; DIR-3 KYC annually.
