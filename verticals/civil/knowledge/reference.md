# Civil-engineering copilot — quick reference (India; indicative only)

> Original summary notes for grounding. All checks are indicative planning aids —
> a licensed engineer must verify loads, codes and site conditions and certify the design.

## Key IS codes
- IS 456:2000 — Plain & Reinforced Concrete (RCC design, limit state).
- IS 800:2007 — General Construction in Steel (limit state).
- IS 875 (Parts 1–5) — Loads (dead, imposed, wind, snow).
- IS 1893 — Earthquake-resistant design (seismic zones, response spectrum).
- IS 1200 — Methods of measurement (for BOQ/measurement sheets).
- SP 16 — Design aids for RCC to IS 456.

## Estimation & costing
- **BOQ**: item description × quantity × unit rate = amount; sum to abstract.
- **Rate analysis**: direct cost (material + labour + equipment) + overhead % + profit %.
- Use CPWD / State PWD **Schedule of Rates (SOR)** for standard unit rates.
- Add wastage allowances; reconcile material consumption against theoretical quantity.

## Concrete (nominal mixes, cement:sand:coarse aggregate)
- M5 1:5:10, M7.5 1:4:8, M10 1:3:6, M15 1:2:4, M20 1:1.5:3, M25 1:1:2.
- Dry volume ≈ 1.54 × wet volume. Cement: 1 bag (50 kg) ≈ 0.0347 m³.
- Above M20, prefer **design mix** over nominal mix.

## Reinforcement
- Bar unit weight = d²/162 kg/m (d in mm). e.g. 12 mm ≈ 0.888 kg/m, 16 mm ≈ 1.58 kg/m.
- Provide cover, development length and lap as per IS 456.

## RCC limit-state quick check (singly reinforced)
- Limiting moment Mu,lim = k·fck·b·d²; k = 0.149 (Fe250), 0.138 (Fe415), 0.133 (Fe500).
- This is an INDICATIVE capacity only — full design covers shear, deflection, detailing, durability.

## Planning
- CPM/PERT for scheduling; identify the critical path; resource-level and track progress (DPR).
